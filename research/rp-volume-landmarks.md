## Findings

### 1. RP publishes volume landmarks as weekly working sets, not as MEV/MAV/MRV alone

RP defines **four** volume landmarks (not three):

| Landmark | Definition |
|----------|-----------|
| **MV** | Maintenance Volume — minimum to keep existing muscle |
| **MEV** | Minimum Effective Volume — minimum to make measurable improvements |
| **MAV** | Maximum Adaptive Volume — range producing best long-term gains |
| **MRV** | Maximum Recoverable Volume — ceiling before recovery fails |

RP also publishes two "priority" variants (MAV\*P and MRV\*P) for when a muscle is prioritized and other training is reduced. These are consistently higher than the standard landmarks.

All values are expressed as **weekly working sets per muscle group** for intermediate lifters (3-7 years training experience).

### 3. Back (Combined) — Lats, Upper Back, Lower Back

RP does **not** publish separate volume landmark tables for Lats, Upper Back, or Lower Back. Instead, RP publishes a single "Back" guide with combined landmarks.

From the RP Help Center (narrative form):

| Landmark | Value | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| MV | ~6 sets/week | Split evenly between vertical and horizontal pulling |
| MEV | ~10 sets/week | |
| MAV (per session) | 4-12 sets | Speculative; research-bounded |
| MRV | 20-35 sets/week | Varies by frequency: 20 (2x/wk), 25 (3x/wk), 30 (4x/wk), 35 (5-6x/wk) |

These values cover the **combination** of vertical and horizontal pulling. RP notes: "when we say that with 4 back sessions a week typical MRVs are around 30 total sets, we generally mean about 15 sets of vertical pulling and 15 sets of rowing, not 30 sets of each."

The blog version of the back article's volume table did not render in text extraction (likely JavaScript-rendered or image-based). The Help Center version provided the narrative numbers above.

**Impact on the 16-group taxonomy:** Three of the 16 muscle groups in `EXERCISE-ATTRIBUTION-REFINEMENT-SCOPE.md` — Lats, Upper Back, and Lower Back — cannot be directly populated from RP's public articles at the granularity assumed. RP treats them as one combined "Back" category.

### 4. Data Format and Machine-Readability

- **Format:** HTML tables embedded in blog articles. Consistent 6-column structure: `MV | MEV | MAV | MRV | MAV*P | MRV*P`.
- **Machine-readable:** Not published as JSON, CSV, or API. Values must be transcribed from HTML tables. Tables are consistent enough for reliable manual transcription.
- **Consistency:** All 13 individual muscle group articles use identical table format and landmark definitions. Highly systematic.

## Contradictions to Current Assumptions

- **Partial contradiction to EXERCISE-ATTRIBUTION-REFINEMENT-SCOPE.md Section 1 taxonomy:** That document assumes RP publishes **separate** volume landmarks for all 16 muscle groups including Lats, Upper Back, and Lower Back individually. RP's public articles treat these three as one combined "Back" category. This does not invalidate the 16-group taxonomy (which has independent justification from the delt split), but it means RP cannot be the sole data source for Lats, Upper Back, and Lower Back individually.
